# Course Outline: Professional Certificate in Digital Marketing

**Level:** Level 5 Diploma
**Credits:** 60
**Duration:** 600 hours

## Course Overview

This programme introduces working professionals to the planning, execution and measurement of digital marketing campaigns across search, social and email channels.

## Purpose

To prepare learners to run integrated digital campaigns for small and medium organisations.

## Target Audience

Early-career marketers and business owners with at least one year of work experience.

## Career Outcomes

- Digital Marketing Executive
- Social Media Manager

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Foundations of Digital Marketing (10 credits)

**Duration:** 100 hours

- The digital marketing landscape
- Customer journeys and funnels
- Marketing channels overview

**Learning Outcomes:**

- Explain the main digital marketing channels
- Map a customer journey for a product
